Slide 01 · PurrfectMatch

Smart, offline cat adoption & shelter management.

A dual-interface desktop solution built to replace fragmented paperwork with a unified, real-time platform for shelter caretakers and caring adopters.

Purpose
Connect homeless pets with families faster by reducing administrative overhead and friction in adoption applications.
Core Principle
Zero-installation, 100% offline-ready operations with resilient local data persistence.
Slide 02 · System Overview

Two tailored surfaces, one unified system.

Designed with strict Object-Oriented principles, separating public discovery from back-office management while sharing live state.

Customer Portal
Public Adopter Interface: Guest catalog browsing, filterable by age and gender, multi-photo carousels (up to 10 photos), and gated quick-adopt applications.
Staff Dashboard
Operations Back-Office: Full pet intake, live webcam photo capture, adopter directories, and single-click adoption approval.
Architecture
OOP Core: Encapsulated domain models, controller orchestration, and thread-safe Singleton database access.
Slide 03 · Technology Stack

Lean, modern, dependency-conscious.

Engineered specifically for fluid desktop performance without heavy runtimes or cloud friction.

Python 3
Core language powering domain models, business logic, and modular controller workflows.
CustomTkinter
Native desktop GUI with dark-themed typography, responsive layouts, and modal controllers.
SQLite 3
Embedded database delivering relational integrity, foreign key cascades, and zero-configuration setup.
OpenCV & Pillow
Live camera hardware streaming for shelter intake combined with thumbnail generation and caching.
